Reference

Trade glossary

TermMeaning
HSHarmonized Commodity Description and Coding System — the WCO's six-digit product nomenclature used by most countries for tariffs and trade statistics.
HTS / HTSUSThe U.S. national tariff schedule, adding digits 7–10 to the HS code.
MFNMost-favoured-nation (“general”) duty rate applied to WTO members without a preference.
AD/CVDAntidumping and countervailing duties — product- and country-specific remedies that stack on top of other duties.
FOBFree On Board (Incoterms®): seller loads goods on the vessel; buyer pays main freight and insurance.
CIFCost, Insurance and Freight: seller pays freight and minimum insurance to the destination port; risk passes at loading.
EXW / DDPEx Works (buyer does almost everything) and Delivered Duty Paid (seller does almost everything, including import duties).
FCL / LCLFull container load vs less-than-container load (shared container priced per m³).
CBMCubic metre — the volume unit for ocean freight. 1 m³ ≈ 35.3 ft³.
MPF / HMFU.S. Merchandise Processing Fee and Harbor Maintenance Fee charged on formal entries.
AQLAcceptable Quality Limit — the statistical sampling standard used in pre-shipment inspections.
KD / RTAKnock-down / ready-to-assemble — furniture shipped in parts to save volume.
Binding rulingA written classification decision from customs that you can rely on for future entries.
DrawbackRefund of duties paid on imported goods that are later exported or destroyed.
