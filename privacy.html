Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains how 000094.com (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit. When you use a form (quote requests, contact, pledges, sponsorship, contest or job applications) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, company, phone and message.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps.
  • Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to us unless you submit a form.
  • Preferences such as theme and cookie choice are stored in your browser's local storage.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request and, for quote requests, to share it with partners relevant to your request so they can contact you (you consent to this when submitting).
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To send newsletters only if you opted in; every email includes an unsubscribe option.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Choosing “Essential only” in our cookie notice requests non-personalised ads.

Service providers

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • FormSubmit — delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts — serves typefaces.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) — videos load only after you click play.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers above, with partners when you request quotes, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep form submissions as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise them, use our contact form. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is intended for adults and business users. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, under appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form.
