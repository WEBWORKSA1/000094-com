Roll-pack mattresses

Compressed foam and hybrid mattresses can cut volume by 70–80%, multiplying units per container.

Knock-down (KD) design

Detachable legs, arms and backs often turn a 1.6 m³ sofa carton into 0.9 m³. Ask suppliers for a KD option.

Mix to fill

Top up a half-empty 40' HC with nightstands or cushions; the marginal freight on those units is close to zero.
