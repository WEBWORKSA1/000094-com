About

Trade intelligence for the things we live with

Sofas, beds, lamps, kitchens and modular homes cross borders in vast volumes, yet the rules that price them are scattered across tariff schedules and legal notices. 000094 puts them in one place, in plain language, for free.

What we do

We publish free tools — a Chapter 94 code explorer, landed-cost and container calculators and a tariff tracker — and practical guides for importers, retailers, designers, manufacturers and students. When readers need hands-on help, our free request service connects them with suppliers, forwarders, customs brokers and inspectors.

Why “000094”?

The name is a zero-padded 94 — a nod to Chapter 94 of the Harmonized System, which covers furniture, bedding, lighting and prefabricated buildings. It is our domain name, not a product code or official designation.

Editorial standards

  • Primary sources first: WCO nomenclature, national tariffs, government proclamations and customs guidance, supported by reputable industry analysis.
  • Dated reviews: pages that describe changing rules carry a “last reviewed” date.
  • Separation: sponsors and partners never edit or approve content. Sponsored placements are labelled.
  • Corrections: spot an error? Tell us via the contact form; we fix verified errors promptly and note material corrections.

How we're funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), labelled sponsorships, referral fees from partners when readers request quotes, and reader donations. Referral fees never change the price a buyer is quoted.

Independence

000094 is independent and is not affiliated with the World Customs Organization, any customs authority or government, or any company mentioned on the site.
