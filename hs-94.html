What's excluded from Chapter 94?

Inflatable & water items

Pneumatic or water mattresses, pillows and cushions are classified by material in Chapters 39, 40 or 63.

Light sources themselves

Bulbs, LED modules and other light sources fall in Chapter 85. Chapter 94 covers the luminaires and fittings.

Toys & game furniture

Toy furniture and toy lamps go to heading 95.03; billiard tables and furniture built for games go to 95.04.

Furniture parts of machines

Cabinets designed as parts of apparatus (for example audio or video equipment) follow that apparatus in Chapter 85.

Clocks & jewellery

Clock cases (Chapter 91) and articles of precious metal (Chapter 71) are excluded.

Stand-alone bedding fabrics

Bed linen and blankets without stuffing are textiles (Chapter 63), not “bedding” of heading 94.04.
